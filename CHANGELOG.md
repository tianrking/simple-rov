# Changelog

## v0.0.1 - 2026-11-14

Initial engineering prototype of SimpleROV.

- Parametric four-thruster mechanics, ten independent printable STL/STEP parts and two motor-fit coupons.
- Standard foam-free assembly with an optional buoyancy module, commercial component references, mechanical BOM and mounting interfaces.
- Illustrated assembly guide, PDF, multi-view CAD gallery and Onshape reference captures.
- MuJoCo 4.0.1 keyboard driving, classical depth/heading/roll stabilization, water-current and tether models, actuator-fault scenarios and reproducible simulation videos.
- English technical documentation with Spanish and Chinese README entry points.
- Digital geometry, software and file-integrity checks. Physical assembly, sealing, trim and water trials remain pending.
- Reserved locations for later KiCad hardware and onboard firmware; neither is implemented in this version.
